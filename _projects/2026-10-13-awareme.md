**AwareMe** is a private, on-device Android app for Nathaniel Branden's sentence-completion exercise. It has no network connection and it is open source, and the code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/nicofirst1/AwareMe). How the exercise works, and why I built the app, is in the blogpost [Finishing Sentences to Know Yourself Better](/blog/awareme/).
