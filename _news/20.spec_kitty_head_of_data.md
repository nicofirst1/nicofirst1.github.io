#### Joining Spec Kitty as Head of Data

I have joined [Spec Kitty](https://spec-kitty.ai/) as Head of Data, alongside my ongoing role at Fraunhofer IAIS. Spec Kitty builds a delivery control plane for AI-assisted software teams, and my job is to make sense of what these systems do: analyzing agentic traces, benchmarking the models and harnesses behind coding agents, and turning that data into decisions the team can act on.
