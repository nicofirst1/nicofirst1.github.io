Speaking

I am Nicolo' Brandizzi, a research scientist at Fraunhofer IAIS. I work on how people and AI systems collaborate: reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and the problem of trusting an agent you did not write yourself. I have worked on it from my PhD through the multi-agent systems I build today, and it sits next to my other role helping coordinate Europe's push to build its own AI infrastructure. What I bring to a talk comes from building these systems and studying them.

What I talk about

Four topics drawn from my research and the systems I build, each one ready as a talk, a podcast, or a workshop.

Talk · podcast · workshop

Orchestrating agents is mostly saying no

Multi-agent systems fail in predictable ways: infinite refinement, agents grading their own work, no place for a human to step in. I build them to stay bounded, with hard iteration caps, deterministic scoring, and a human gate. It draws on my PhD in reinforcement learning, an AFOSR-funded project (US Air Force research) on trust in human-AI teams, and Klarbot, a system I built with those limits designed in.

Good fit for AI-engineering and agentic-AI podcasts, LLMOps conferences, applied-ML audiences.

Talk · podcast

Teaching machines to think

Reinforcement learning gets the credit for teaching language models to reason. I think that is too generous: some of what looks like new reasoning is the model surfacing what it already knew. This talk separates where RL's gains are real from where the story gets oversold, in plain language from my own field.

Good fit for ML research podcasts, university seminars, technical AI audiences.

Talk · panel

Europe's AI Factories, from the inside

Europe is spending billions to build its own AI compute and data infrastructure. I co-wrote one of those proposals, the €25M JUPITER AI Factory, and I also ran the numbers and found large overlaps between projects meant to complement each other. So I can argue both sides: why sovereign infrastructure matters, and why it fragments without something like a CERN for AI to coordinate it.

Good fit for EU policy and AI-sovereignty panels, EuroHPC and research-funding events, European tech-strategy shows.

Workshop · talk

Data quality for AI, from a 700-terabyte pipeline

AI projects often fail before training starts, on data problems nobody measured. I co-led the data work for a multilingual European LLM, a 700-terabyte pipeline across 24 languages, and I now teach it to research teams. The workshop is practical: how to define quality, where it breaks, and how to test a data-quality tool instead of trusting it at face value.

Good fit for data teams, internal trainings, public-sector digitization, universities.
