## What This App Does

This application is used to create, review, and publish content workflows, including posting content to LinkedIn after user authorization.

## Information We Collect

We may collect and process:

- Basic account information made available through LinkedIn authorization
- Access tokens or related authorization data required to post on the user's behalf
- Content the user chooses to create, edit, approve, or publish
- Technical logs needed to operate, secure, and debug the service

## How We Use Information

We use information only to:

- authenticate the user with LinkedIn
- enable requested publishing actions
- operate and maintain the service
- diagnose failures and improve reliability
- protect the service against misuse

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We do not share data with third parties except:

- when required to operate the service through authorized platform APIs such as LinkedIn
- when required by law
- when necessary to protect the security or integrity of the service

## Data Retention

We keep data only for as long as needed to operate the service, maintain logs, and support troubleshooting or compliance needs.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored data and credentials. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## User Control

Users may revoke LinkedIn access at any time through their LinkedIn account settings or by contacting us.

## Children's Privacy

This service is not directed to children.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted at this URL.
